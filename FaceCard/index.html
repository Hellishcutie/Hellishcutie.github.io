The Practice

A reading drawn from life

Face Card by Vera is a live portrait and tarot reading service offered at markets, pop-ups, and psychic faires across Los Angeles. In a single sitting, the cards are drawn, the portrait begins, and what emerges in the drawing informs what is spoken aloud.

The result is yours to keep — a sketch of you rendered as the archetype the cards already knew you were. No two readings look alike. No two portraits do either.

Optional: share your sun, moon, and rising at the time of your sitting to add another layer to your reading.

What to expect

How a sitting works

i

The cards are drawn

You shuffle or cut the deck. The cards that surface are yours. A spread is laid and read aloud — the archetypes, the suit, the story between them.

ii

The portrait begins

As pen meets paper, so does the reading. What emerges in your face informs what is said, and what is said shapes what is drawn. The two happen together.

iii

You leave with both

The sketch is yours. The reading lives with you. The card's suit determines the palette — cups in blue, wands in red, pentacles in green, swords in purple, majors by the card itself.
